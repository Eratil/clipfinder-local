Detailed scoring

These scores explain the selected clip. They do not replace your approval decision.

Legend

Suggested score
Based on your active discovery profile, prompt/reference similarity, previous approvals and rejections, and relevant reactions from game, voice and chat.
Quality
Rewards natural speaking pace, usable clip length, emotion or a verified game-to-voice reaction. Reading cues, static text-heavy game screens and incomplete speech lower it.
Short potential
Rewards a short-friendly length, a clear standalone thought, a hook, payoff, emotion and chat engagement. It is strongly reduced by reading aloud, very long clips or missing context.
Context
Compares the surrounding speech before and after the fragment. A score falls when the clip begins or ends in the middle of a thought.
Self-contained
Measures whether a new viewer can understand the clip without previous conversation. Complete sentences and an understandable point increase it.
Extended completeness
Available after Extended analysis. It performs an extra check of sentence boundaries, a complete ending and whether the clip reaches its point or punchline.
